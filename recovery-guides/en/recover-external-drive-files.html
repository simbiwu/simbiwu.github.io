What to do when files disappear from an external drive

Check whether the external drive is stable before choosing read-only scanning or stopping for professional recovery.

Last reviewed 2026-08-11

Direct answer

Stop copies and repair commands. Scan read-only only when capacity and reads are stable. Stop powering a drive that disconnects, clicks, or freezes the system.

Separate logical and hardware problems

Accidental deletion, formatting, and directory damage may be software-recovery cases. Repeated disconnects, mechanical noises, unstable capacity, or system freezes during reads point toward hardware trouble.

Handle a stable device

When the device is consistently recognized, scan the relevant volume read-only. Check file-system records first and use a deep scan only when needed. Recover to an internal drive or another healthy disk.

Avoid repair, defragmentation, or formatting operations that write to the source.

Stop when the device is abnormal

Repeated power cycles and full scans can worsen a mechanically failing drive. Important data should go to a professional service with hardware capabilities. Super Recovery does not repair hardware or rebuild arrays.

Common question

Can I scan a drive that has a letter but will not open?

Only when its capacity and connection remain stable and there are no unusual noises. Stop if the device is unstable.
