How to recover files after emptying the Recycle Bin

Windows no longer offers direct restore after the Recycle Bin is emptied. Stop writing to the original drive, scan that volume, and save results elsewhere.

How to recover deleted or missing files from a USB drive

Disconnect the USB drive to prevent further writes. Reconnect it only when ready, scan the whole device, and recover files to a computer drive.

How to recover files after a quick format

Do not format again or write new data. Scan the whole formatted volume, inspect recognizable folders first, then use a deep scan and recover elsewhere.

What to do when files disappear from an external drive

Stop copies and repair commands. Scan read-only only when capacity and reads are stable. Stop powering a drive that disconnects, clicks, or freezes the system.

Read guide → 06

How to recover files from a drive shown as RAW

RAW means Windows cannot recognize the current file system. Do not format first; scan the stable volume and save candidates elsewhere.

Why SSD TRIM affects deleted-file recovery

TRIM tells an SSD that blocks are no longer needed. Once the controller clears them, recovery software cannot read the old content, so stop writes quickly.

What not to do after losing files

Do not keep using, format, repair, or defragment the source drive. Assess its stability, scan read-only, and recover to another drive.
