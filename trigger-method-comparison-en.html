Which privacy trigger fits your situation?

First ask whether you can naturally interact with the PC. Shortcuts, gestures, and USB removal are active triggers. Bluetooth disconnect and away detection work better after you have already left.

Two different needs

“No time to act” is not the same as “forgot to act”

Someone approaches

Protection must start now. Prefer a shortcut, one-click, gesture, or USB removal.

You have left

A phone or headset moves away with you, or the camera confirms that you are away.

Practical combinations

One primary trigger plus one backup

  1. Regular office useUse a shortcut first and Bluetooth disconnect when you forget.
  2. Frequent sudden departuresUse a personal USB device first and away detection as backup.
  3. Conspicuous input is difficultPrepare a gesture and use Bluetooth after you have left.
