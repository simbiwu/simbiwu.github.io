Use cases

A follow-up after leaving, not an emergency button

01

Headset moves with you

You leave for a call or meeting while still wearing the headset.

02

Phone stays with you

The phone can provide a useful presence reference when you leave the desk.

03

You forgot to lock

No action was taken before leaving, so disconnection provides a backup.

Before enabling

Test the device, distance, and delay

  1. Choose a stable deviceUse a phone or headset you normally carry away.
  2. Set disconnect conditionsChoose a delay that reduces reactions to brief connection changes.
  3. Walk through the scenarioTest at your usual desk before relying on it.

Difference

Keep an active trigger for urgent moments

Bluetooth disconnect

For when you have already left; timing can vary with the device and environment.

Shortcut, gesture, or one-click

For when someone approaches and protection must start immediately.

Protection actions

Disconnection starts only what you selected

Begin with a clear action such as locking Windows, then add file-vault locking if it fits your routine.
