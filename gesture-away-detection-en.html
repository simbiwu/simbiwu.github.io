Core difference

Active gesture and automatic away detection

Gesture trigger

When someone approaches, perform a prepared gesture to start protection without obvious keyboard or mouse input.

Away detection

Protection starts after you leave and the conditions you selected are met.

How to choose

Do you need protection now or after leaving?

01

Someone walks over

Use gesture to control the exact moment.

02

You must stand up now

Away detection can provide a follow-up after departure.

03

Both happen

Enable each as needed and choose suitable away conditions.

Before use

Test in your real lighting and seating position

  1. Check the cameraGrant the required permission in Windows and the app.
  2. Adjust conditionsMatch the gesture or away settings to the desk and lighting.
  3. Test actionsConfirm trigger timing before adding more actions.

On-device analysis: Camera frames used for gesture and away decisions are not saved or uploaded as photos or video.

Alternatives

The camera is not your only option

If the device, lighting, or seating does not suit camera triggers, use a shortcut, one-click, USB removal, or Bluetooth disconnect instead.
