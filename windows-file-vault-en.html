Current screen

Vault contents

Lock open vaults. Without the password, they cannot be reopened to view the contents.

Before use

Keep the password and recovery material safe

The vault combines Windows VHDX, NTFS, and BitLocker capabilities. Recovery material can be important if the password is forgotten.

Does the vault upload files?

No. The vault protects local files; creating or using one does not upload its contents.

Can files be viewed after the vault is locked?

No. The vault password is required to open it again.

Does a vault replace backups?

No. It protects access. Important files still need reliable backups.
