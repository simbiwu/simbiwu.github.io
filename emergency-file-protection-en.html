Scenario guide · Urgent protection

How to protect your privacy when someone approaches your PC

A colleague may walk up to your desk, a roommate may enter unexpectedly, or a visitor may arrive beside your PC. You may have no time to switch windows, and reaching for the keyboard or mouse may be inconvenient or conspicuous. Useful protection must be prepared before that moment.

The real problem is more than forgetting to lock the PC

No time to close everything

Chats, contracts, photos, or customer documents may be open at the same time. Organizing every window after someone arrives is already too late.

Normal interaction is inconvenient

A sudden mouse movement, key combination, or closing the laptop may look deliberate, and your hands may not be near the keyboard.

Prepare one fast trigger that feels natural

File vaults protect long-term storage; fast triggers handle the moment

Files that need ongoing protection

Contracts, photos, and personal documents can be placed in a local file vault beforehand. They remain locked while the vault is closed.

Content currently visible on screen

After a trigger, SuperWatchDog follows the protection actions selected in advance, so you do not need to configure anything during the urgent moment.

Prepare just three things in advance

  1. Place local files that need ongoing protection in a file vault.
  2. Choose a gesture, one-click action, or keyboard shortcut as your main fast trigger.
  3. Try it on your own PC and confirm that the actions, countdown, and timing match your routine.

Gesture triggers require an available camera and must be enabled by the user. Bluetooth disconnect and away detection are better suited as follow-up options after you forget to act or have already left. Configure actions that may affect unsaved work carefully, and keep vault passwords and recovery materials safe.
