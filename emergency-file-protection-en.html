Get started

Prepare three things

  1. Store filesPlace local files that need ongoing protection in a vault.
  2. Choose a triggerPick one primary trigger that feels natural.
  3. Try it firstConfirm the actions, countdown, and timing fit your routine.
What does gesture triggering require?

An available camera and explicit user activation are required.

Can protection actions affect unsaved work?

Actions that close windows, lock the PC, or restart it should be selected as needed and tried before regular use.

What if I forget a vault password?

Keep the password and recovery materials safe so recovery remains available when needed.
