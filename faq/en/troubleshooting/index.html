Troubleshooting and safety

Handle missed triggers, Bluetooth false disconnects, forceful actions, and forgotten vault passwords.

4 questions · Last reviewed 2026-07-31

What should I check first when a trigger does not protect the PC?

Confirm that the trigger is enabled and its device or camera is available, then use manual Protect Now to test the action itself. Review the in-app trigger history and understandable error message afterward.

Why is a nearby Bluetooth device reported as disconnected?

Bluetooth can briefly disconnect because of device sleep, phone background restrictions, radio interference, or the edge of signal range. Observe the pattern, then allow more confirmation time or choose a more stable device.

Read the full answer →

Can force-lock, restart, or shutdown affect unsaved files?

Yes. Force-close, force-lock or unmount, restart, and shutdown can interrupt applications and lose unsaved content. They should remain disabled by default and be used only when you accept the consequences.

Read the full answer →

What should I do if I forget the vault password?

Stop repeated guesses and any operation that could overwrite the original vault. Locate the recovery material saved during creation and follow the recovery flow. Decryption should not be promised without the password or valid recovery material.
