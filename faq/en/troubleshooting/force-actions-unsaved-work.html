Troubleshooting and safety

Can force-lock, restart, or shutdown affect unsaved files?

Short answer

Yes. Force-close, force-lock or unmount, restart, and shutdown can interrupt applications and lose unsaved content. They should remain disabled by default and be used only when you accept the consequences.

High-impact actions do not replace saving

An application may be writing a document, database, or file inside the vault. Sudden termination or storage removal can discard incomplete writes, and some files may require the application's recovery process.

Prefer window hiding and Windows lock. If a stronger action is truly needed, narrow its scenario, allow more confirmation time, and rehearse with disposable data. Maintain an independent backup for important data.

  • Keep forceful actions disabled by default
  • Save and close files in use
  • Test with disposable data
  • Maintain an independent backup
