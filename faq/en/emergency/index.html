Emergency screen privacy

Choose a practical first action when someone approaches, you cannot reach the computer, or current content must be covered quickly.

4 questions · Last reviewed 2026-07-31

What should I do first when someone suddenly approaches my PC?

Use the fastest reliable action with the least impact: hide windows or lock Windows when you are at the keyboard; rely on a preconfigured device or camera trigger only after you have stepped away.

Read the full answer →

How can I protect privacy when I cannot reach the keyboard or mouse?

You need a trigger that was enabled and tested in advance, such as removing a USB device, moving a Bluetooth device out of range, or using a camera gesture. Without prior setup, software cannot infer your intent in the moment.

Read the full answer →

In an urgent moment, should I lock Windows or only hide windows?

Hide windows for brief visual cover. Lock Windows whenever someone might continue using the PC. Neither action replaces encryption for sensitive files.

Read the full answer →

How can I protect privacy without losing unsaved work?

Use window hiding or Windows lock as the first layer, and leave force-close, restart, and shutdown actions disabled. Important documents should still rely on the application's save and recovery features.
