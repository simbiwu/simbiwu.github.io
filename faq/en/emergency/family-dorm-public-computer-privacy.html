Emergency screen privacy

How can I stop private content being seen at home, in a dorm, or in public?

Short answer

Start with screen placement, hidden notification previews, and Windows lock, then prepare one familiar quick trigger. In shared environments, do not rely only on closing a window; lock the PC whenever you step away.

Everyday environment habits matter more than one action

Adjust the screen angle, remove sensitive previews from the lock screen and desktop, and use separate Windows accounts for different people. In dorms and public spaces, lock the computer even for a short absence.

Quick hiding can cover the moment when someone approaches, but the PC may remain usable. Store private files in an appropriate protected location and follow the rules of the device and location.

  • Disable sensitive notification previews
  • Lock Windows whenever you leave
  • Use separate accounts on a shared PC
