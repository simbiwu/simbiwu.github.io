Emergency screen privacy

How can I protect office privacy when a manager suddenly walks over?

Short answer

Choose window hiding or Windows lock according to the sensitivity and avoid force-closing applications in a panic. SuperWatchDog does not decide what you are protecting; you choose the trigger and actions in advance.

Balance office privacy with work continuity

For brief visual privacy, use a configured trigger to hide selected windows. Lock Windows if someone may use the PC. Client records, contracts, and quotations must also follow workplace policies and access rules.

The software does not classify content as work, personal, or anything else, and it does not judge the people nearby. Users choose the target windows, trigger, and actions, and should use them only for lawful personal privacy and information protection.

  • Brief cover: hide selected windows
  • Leaving or possible access: lock Windows
  • Important records: also follow organizational data rules
