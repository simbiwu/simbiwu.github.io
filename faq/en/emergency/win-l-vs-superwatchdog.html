Emergency screen privacy

What is the difference between Win+L and SuperWatchDog?

Short answer

Win+L is the direct built-in shortcut for locking Windows and works well when you can reach the keyboard. SuperWatchDog is for users who want other trigger methods and their own combination of window, lock-screen, and vault actions.

Win+L is the baseline; alternative triggers help beyond keyboard reach

When your hand is near the keyboard, Win+L is often fast enough and needs no extra setup. It locks the current Windows session, but does not decide which windows to handle or necessarily lock a private file container that remains open.

SuperWatchDog lets users configure a shortcut, device, or camera trigger and choose actions such as hiding windows, locking Windows, or protecting a vault. It is not a mandatory replacement for Win+L; the right choice depends on distance, environment, and acceptable impact.

  • At the keyboard: remember Win+L
  • For touch-free or device triggers: configure and test first
  • For files: verify the vault state separately
