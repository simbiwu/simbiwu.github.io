Emergency screen privacy

How can I quickly protect my screen when someone walks up to my PC?

Short answer

Use the fastest reliable action with the least impact: hide windows or lock Windows when you are at the keyboard; rely on a preconfigured device or camera trigger only after you have stepped away.

Choose by distance first

A shortcut is usually the quickest option while your hand is near the keyboard. Hide or minimize windows for brief visual cover; lock Windows if the other person may touch the PC.

Once you are away, it is too late to configure a trigger. USB removal, Bluetooth disconnect, and away detection should be set up and tried before an urgent moment.

  • Brief visual cover: hide or minimize current windows
  • Prevent further use: lock Windows
  • Sensitive files: keep them in a locked vault as well
