Choosing a trigger

Compare shortcuts, gestures, USB removal, and Bluetooth triggers for different distances and environments.

6 questions · Last reviewed 2026-08-02

Should I use a shortcut, USB, Bluetooth, or a camera trigger?

Use a shortcut while at the PC, USB when you can take a device with you, Bluetooth when a personal device leaves with you, and a gesture when you remain in view but cannot use your hands.

Which is better for urgent triggering: a shortcut or a camera gesture?

Prefer a shortcut when the keyboard is within reach. A gesture is useful when your hands are occupied or the keyboard is farther away while you remain in camera view. Practice both before relying on them.

When is USB removal a useful privacy trigger?

It suits people who use one designated USB device as a physical trigger: take it when leaving to run the selected protection. It does not encrypt files on that USB device, and removing an arbitrary USB device does not trigger protection.

How can I reduce false triggers from Bluetooth disconnects?

Choose a stable device that actually leaves with you, allow time for brief connection drops, and start with a reversible action such as Windows lock. Do not let occasionally sleeping headphones directly restart or shut down the PC.

Read the full answer →

What is the difference between a camera gesture and away detection?

A gesture is an explicit command while you are in view. Away detection acts after continued absence and helps when you forget, but it needs more conservative timing.

Read the full answer →

Why does Bluetooth 'connected' not prove a device is next to the PC?

Connected means that a logical link exists; it does not prove an exact physical distance. Walls, the human body, transmit power, power-saving behavior, and radio interference all affect connection state and signal strength.
