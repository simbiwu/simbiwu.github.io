Choosing a trigger

How can I reduce false triggers from Bluetooth disconnects?

Short answer

Choose a stable device that actually leaves with you, allow time for brief connection drops, and start with a reversible action such as Windows lock. Do not let occasionally sleeping headphones directly restart or shut down the PC.

A disconnect does not always mean you left

Headphone power saving, phone restrictions, radio interference, and distance changes can all cause a brief disconnect. Observe the device in your normal environment before treating it as a reliable presence signal.

If the environment is unstable, use a reasonable delay or switch to USB or a shortcut. Trigger history can help distinguish leaving from connection trouble, but responsiveness should not make everyday work unreliable.

  • Prefer a stable device that stays with you
  • Allow brief disconnects to recover
  • Keep high-impact actions disabled while tuning
