Choosing a trigger

Which is better for urgent triggering: a shortcut or a camera gesture?

Short answer

Prefer a shortcut when the keyboard is within reach. A gesture is useful when your hands are occupied or the keyboard is farther away while you remain in camera view. Practice both before relying on them.

Reliability comes from matching the situation

A shortcut does not depend on lighting or camera view and is usually more predictable, but you must reach the keyboard and remember the keys. A gesture is touch-free but depends on lighting, occlusion, position, and camera availability.

Do not bind a complex action chain to an unfamiliar gesture. Start with a reversible result such as hiding windows or locking Windows, then use it routinely after recognition is stable.

  • Shortcuts suit close range
  • Gestures suit touch-free use while visible
  • Keep another manual way to recover
