Private file protection

How should I protect private files and accounts before lending my PC?

Short answer

Give the borrower a separate Windows account or guest environment, sign out of accounts they do not need, and lock the private-file vault. Do not rely only on moving files to another folder or hiding windows.

Separate accounts and files before lending

Review browser sync, email, chat, cloud storage, the desktop, and Downloads. Disable autofill and sensitive notification previews. When possible, create a restricted local account so the borrower does not enter your everyday session.

Use appropriate encryption for private files that remain on the PC and verify that the vault is locked. SuperWatchDog can run user-selected protection actions, but it does not automatically identify private files or accounts.

  • Use a separate restricted Windows account
  • Sign out of applications and web accounts that are not needed
  • Lock the vault and keep backups of important data
