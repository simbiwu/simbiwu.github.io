Smart trigger · USB removal

Take the linked device and leave now

If you already carry a USB device away, link it in advance. Removing it can immediately start the protection flow you selected.

Use cases

For “I am taking this device and leaving now”

01

Called away suddenly

There is no time to close or organize every window.

02

The device is personal

A drive, receiver, or dongle normally moves with you.

03

You want a clear trigger

Removal is an explicit action with no distance condition to wait for.

Preparation

Link, test, then enable

  1. Choose a deviceUse one that stays under your control and is not removed casually.
  2. Select actionsChoose Windows lock, window handling, and vault locking as needed.
  3. Simulate leavingRemove it once and confirm both the trigger and result.

Difference

USB removal is active; Bluetooth follows after leaving

USB removal

The timing is explicit: you decide that protection starts now.

Bluetooth disconnect

No action is required at the desk, so it is useful after you forget.

Get started

Do not link a device other people frequently move

Use a stable device you control. For the first test, begin with a clear action such as locking Windows.
